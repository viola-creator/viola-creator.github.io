About the suite

The largest of the three

A secluded entryway opens onto a contemporary space where you forget you are in a machiya. The dining room overlooks a modern tokonoma displaying time-worn antiques, and a moody reading nook invites you to unwind at the end of the day.

The master en-suite bedroom sits on the second floor. The tatami room is hand-wrapped in Japanese washi by artisan Hatano Wataru. The main bathroom has a large walk-in shower and a custom bathtub handcrafted by Shigaraki ceramic sculptors.

The Story
Architecture · 建築

Designed by Uoya Shigenori

Maana Kiyomizu was designed by Kyoto architect Uoya Shigenori as a modern take on the machiya. It opened in 2022 with three suites, the Maana Living shop, and Kissa Kishin café under one roof.

Interior · 室礼

Serene, light-filled, intimate

The three suites are serene, light-filled and artfully intimate. Antiques and modern crafts live side by side, showing the aesthetics of different eras. An unexpected approach to the machiya, the suites offer comfortable seating areas, relaxation tubs, and queen-size western beds.

Every suite has Japanese artisanal crafts from POJ Studio, original Japanese towels, Wyphyto bath amenities, a bath ritual set, an artisan tea set, and seasonal bath salts.
